# 29 September — real data and model comparison

Six real red-ring recordings were exported; two expected episodes were absent. Episodes 0–3 trained the models, 4–5 were held out.

## Repairs and signals

Five zero-state rows were filled without changing images or indices. Vector statistics were recomputed; two varying right-arm channels had incorrectly recorded zero standard deviation.

Gripper state copied its command in 100% of frames, while arm values were not exact copies. The recorded gripper command stayed at or below 0.94.

## ACT short run

ACT trained 3,000 steps × batch 8 over 3,388 training frames, about seven passes. The complete five-camera run took 42.5 minutes with the viewer also running.

| Condition | Timing hits | False alarms | Grasp error | Motion skill | Arm error over 1 s |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hold / test | 0% | 0% | 0.5° | 0.00 | 2.4° |
| ACT / training | 26% | 0% | 2.2° | +0.23 | 2.1° |
| ACT / test | 17% | 22% | 18.4° | −2.86 | 11.1° |

Test motion skill improved slightly from −3.45 at 1,000 steps to −3.01 at 2,000 and −2.86 at 3,000; earlier stopping did not explain the gap. Loss fell from 14.7 to 0.63 while unseen behaviour remained worse than hold.

The training timing figure split into 75% for ≤0.3 s lead and 26% overall. Around 0.2 s lead it hit 7 of 10 switches; at 0.6–0.8 s lead it hit none of 20.

## SmolVLA alongside ACT

| Model | Train arm error | Test arm error | Train timing, all / short | Test timing, all / short |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ACT, 3,000 × 8 | 2.1° | 11.1° | 26% / 75% | 17% / 33% |
| SmolVLA, 7,500 × 32 | 0.4° | 6.0° | 100% / 100% | 50% / 67% |

SmolVLA learned the training gripper switches, including long lead times. The signal supports learning. The held-out gap narrowed but remained worse than hold; more demonstration variation remains necessary.

This was not an equal-budget architecture trial: ACT saw 24,000 samples from scratch and five cameras; SmolVLA saw 240,000 from pretrained weights and three cameras. Two test episodes contained only twelve gripper events.

## Evaluator corrections

Idle arm groups were excluded from motion ratios, grasp error changed to absolute degrees, and short-lead timing became a separate reported score. [Current evaluation definitions](../../learning/evaluation.md).
