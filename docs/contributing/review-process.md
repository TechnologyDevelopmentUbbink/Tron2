# Review Process

Every page should have a visible status that reflects both editorial maturity and technical evidence.

| Status | Meaning | Exit criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Content is being assembled and may be incomplete. | Structure and sources are ready for review. |
| **Technical review** | A qualified reviewer is checking technical accuracy, safety, sources, and versions. | Review comments are resolved and evidence is traceable. |
| **Verified** | Technical content has been confirmed against an official source or an appropriately recorded Ubbink test. | Verification scope and applicable versions are recorded. |
| **Published** | Reviewed content is available on the documentation website. | The merged page builds cleanly and remains correctly classified. |
| **Needs update** | Published content may be outdated, incomplete, or affected by a version change. | Content is corrected and passes review again. |

## Workflow

1. The author marks new material **Draft** and assigns source classifications.
2. A reviewer checks claims, procedures, safety wording, links, and version scope.
3. The author resolves findings and records source or test evidence.
4. The reviewer marks qualifying content **Verified**.
5. The change is merged and becomes **Published** through the normal repository workflow.
6. Anyone finding stale information marks it **Needs update** and records the reason.

Technical verification should be performed by someone other than the original author whenever practical.
